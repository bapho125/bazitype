About

BaZi Type is a small project run from Japan (contact: hello@bazitype.com). We read your BaZi chart (Four Pillars, calculated for Singapore time) and name one pattern you tend to repeat: a strength you lean on a little too often. Then we give you one small thing to try this month.

How we make it. The calculation is done by a fixed program, not by AI, and it runs in your own browser. The texts for the 50 patterns were written with the help of AI and checked by us. Everyone with the same pattern sees the same text, and we say so.

What we don't do. We don't predict events or promise results. We don't give medical, legal or financial advice.

If something in your life feels too heavy to carry alone, please talk to a professional you trust — a counsellor or a doctor. A chart can help you notice a habit; it can't replace a person who can listen. In Singapore, Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) is there 24 hours: call 1767 or WhatsApp 9151 1767.

How we count your pattern

BaZi schools differ in several places. Here is exactly what we do, so you can check it.

  1. Your chart. Year, month, day and hour pillars from your Singapore clock time (with the old UTC+7:30 and other offsets before 1982). The year and month change at the solar terms (Li Chun on about 4 February), not at Lunar New Year.
  2. What we count. The seven characters other than your Day Master (five if you don't know your birth time). Each heavenly stem counts 1 point. Each earthly branch is split into its hidden stems.
  3. The month counts double. The month branch holds the most seasonal strength, so its hidden stems count ×2.
  4. Ten Gods into five groups. Each character becomes one of the Ten Gods as seen from your Day Master, and the Ten Gods are added into five groups: Peers 比劫, Output 食伤, Wealth 财, Officer 官杀, Resource 印. The biggest group, together with your Day Master, names your pattern (10 × 5 = 50 patterns).
  5. Ties. If two groups are equal, we prefer the group of the month branch's main stem, then the group that shows more often in the stems, then the one concentrated in a single Ten God. If the top two are within 0.5 points, we show both ("between two patterns").
  6. No birth time. We try all 12 possible birth hours and take the most common pattern. If not all 12 agree, we tell you, and show the other pattern. With a rough time (morning, afternoon, evening, night) we try the 3 hours inside it.
