Privacy

Your birth date stays in your browser. The calculation runs on your own device. We do not send or store the date or time you enter.

What we count. To learn whether this actually helps people, the page may send a few anonymous taps to a Google Form we own (the answers land in our own spreadsheet): your pattern number (1–50), the answers you tap (for example "Partly", "Work", "Not yet"), which buttons you press (a move, a reminder, share, the notebook), which link brought you here (for example a shared card, Pinterest, our Etsy shop or an ad), whether you tapped a link to our Etsy shop, whether the browser says it is run by a program (so we can leave out bots), your device's time zone and language setting (for example "Asia/Singapore", "en-SG" — so we can tell whether people in Singapore are using it), and the time. We never send your birth date, birth time, name or email. Google receives these taps like any web request; we do not receive your IP address or location. We don't use advertising cookies.

Reminders. "Remind me in 7 days" makes a calendar file on your own device, or opens Google Calendar with the reminder filled in (your move and a link with your pattern number). If you save it in Google Calendar, Google's privacy policy applies there.

Sharing. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with a short message you can edit. We don't see who you send it to.

Email. Sign-up is not open yet, so no address is sent or stored. When it opens, this page will name the email service we use and what it keeps.

The notebook. It isn't on sale yet. "Tell me when it's ready" only counts the tap; it doesn't take any contact details.
